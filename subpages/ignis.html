The Knights of Unity

Ignis: Duels of Wizards

Ignis: Duels of Wizards is a multiplatform 1v1 MOBA developed by The Knights of Unity. It is in late development and has yet to be released on Steam.

The game features arena combat between a variety of characters with unique spells, summons, and fighting mechanics. Players can choose between online matchmaking, local multiplayer, and solo matches against AI.

My Contributions

My time with Ignis: Duels of Wizards was fairly limited. During the few months I worked on the project, I focused primarily on UI development: matchmaking, menus and settings, controller support, keyboard navigation, and local multiplayer support with split-screen.

Working on a multiplayer title gave me hands-on experience with matchmaking interfaces and the UI and input requirements of local split-screen play.
