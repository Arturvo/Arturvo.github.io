Gas Station Simulator - Tidal Wave DLC

Tidal Wave is a DLC for Gas Station Simulator set on a remote tropical island. Players establish and manage a gas station serving cars, boats, and jet skis.

The island introduces its own rules and new gameplay mechanics, including appeasing a local deity, within a setting distinct from Route 66.

My Contributions

Tidal Wave DLC was the first major project I worked on at Drago Entertainment. I was responsible for features of moderate scope, including the boat movement system and boat-related minigames. I also implemented a range of fun secondary features, such as rocket jumping and player interactions with water and lava.

Below are the key features and areas I was responsible for:

Boat Movement System

  • Implemented a system for defining routes for boats to follow.
  • Integrated the Oceanology plugin into the project and configured it to interact with the boats.
  • Fine-tuned the physics configuration and added custom checks and corrections to keep boats floating realistically across different frame rates.

Boat Fuelling

  • Adapted the existing fuelling minigame for boats.
  • Implemented custom customer scheduling to keep the station running smoothly.
  • Added a new employee role to automate boat fuelling.

Rocket Jump

  • Implemented a playful side mechanic that lets players launch themselves with the anti-shark cannon, offering an alternative way to travel around the gas station.
  • Fine-tuned the physics parameters to make rocket jumping fun and silly while remaining a useful way to get around.
