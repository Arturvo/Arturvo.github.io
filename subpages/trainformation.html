TrainFormation

TrainFormation is a simple, fast-paced 3D puzzle game for PC, developed solo in Unity and released for free on Steam. The goal is to arrange hexagonal railway tiles in front of an ever-accelerating train and travel as far as possible.

The game features varied procedurally generated levels, power-ups, multiple difficulty settings, and achievements. It has been played by over 1,000 players on Steam and has received very positive reviews.

Features I am most proud of

Solo Steam Release

  • Created a Steam page from scratch, including the trailer, description, GIFs, and screenshots.
  • Published the game through the complete Steam release process.
  • Implemented Steam achievements.

Procedurally Generated Terrain

  • Developed a system for generating hex-based procedural terrain with dynamic transitions between different hex elevations.
  • Optimized terrain generation using HLSL compute shaders to support real-time generation.
  • Wrote custom vertex and fragment shaders to light the terrain.
  • Implemented procedural texture generation based on designer settings and terrain vertex properties.

Procedurally Generated Levels

  • Developed an algorithm for generating an endless stream of levels with varying properties.
  • Combined pseudorandom hex and track generation with constraints that keep the frequency of each track type within defined ranges and ensure every level can be completed.
  • Made levels progressively more difficult by introducing new terrain types and elevations, while adjusting the frequency and types of available tracks.
