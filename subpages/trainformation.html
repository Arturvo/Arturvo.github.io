TrainFormation

TrainFormation is a simple, fast-paced 3D puzzle game for PC, developed solo in Unity and released for free on Steam. The goal is to arrange hexagonal railway tiles in front of an ever-accelerating train and travel as far as possible.

The game features varied procedurally generated levels, power-ups, multiple difficulty settings, and achievements. It has been played by over 1,000 players on Steam and has received very positive reviews.
